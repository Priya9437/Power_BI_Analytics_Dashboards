# 📊 Power BI Executive Analytics Dashboards (End-to-End Portfolio)

## 📌 Project Overview
This repository showcases my top corporate-level **Power BI Business Intelligence** dashboards. The primary focus of these projects is to transform raw, complex transactional data into interactive, stakeholder-ready visual analytics that track dynamic revenue metrics, monitor product inventory, and optimize business operations.

---

## 🛍️ Project 1: E-Commerce Sales Dashboard
### 🔍 Key Metrics & Insights Solved:
* **Revenue Trajectory:** Tracked monthly and quarterly sales performance to identify peak revenue periods.
* **Product Performance:** Analyzed top-performing product categories and sub-categories driving maximum profit margins.
* **Regional Insights:** Mapped out geographical sales distribution to help target local marketing campaigns.
* **Customer Dynamics:** Segmented order volumes based on delivery status and consumer payment methods.

## 📱 Project 2: Samsung Sales Dashboard
### 🔍 Key Metrics & Insights Solved:
* **Channel Distribution:** Evaluated product supply chains and sales volume across various wholesale and retail channels.
* **Device Rating Analysis:** Correlated customer satisfaction ratings with specific device models to pinpoint product performance issues.
* **Profit Margin Optimization:** Modeled manufacturing costs against net sales to uncover hidden leaks in product margins.
* **Inventory Control:** Provided dynamic time-intelligence tracking for stock flow and order delivery times.
